    § 02  Questions

    Before you write

    Short answers about replies, the free consultation and what to send.

    All questions
    Q.01How fast will you respond?

    We reply within one business day. Our hours are Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 5 PM Central, so a request sent on a weekend is answered on the next business day.

    Critical support requests are acknowledged within two hours during business hours. For those, call (715) 255-0420 rather than using the form.

    Q.02Is the first consultation really free?

    Yes. The initial consultation costs nothing and doesn’t commit you to anything. If you want to go ahead, you get a written scope of work and a fixed-price quote, with any Texas sales tax shown as its own line, before any work begins. No hidden fees.

    Q.03What should I include in my request?

    What isn’t working or what you want built, roughly how many people, devices or rooms are involved, where the work is, and any deadline. A photo of an error message or a list of your equipment helps too, and you can send those by email.

    Please don’t send passwords, account numbers or other sensitive details through the form or by email. If the job needs access to a system, we’ll arrange it with you directly.

    Q.04Do you come to us, or work remotely?

    Both. We work on-site across Dallas–Fort Worth from our base in Rowlett. Database and web work can be done remotely for clients anywhere. See our service areas for the cities we cover.
