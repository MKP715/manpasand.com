Privacy policy

What our website and contact form collect, what we do with it, who else handles it and how to ask us to delete it. The short version: very little, used only to answer you and do the work you ask for, and we never sell it.

Privacy policyUpdated Nov 3, 2026

The short version

  • This website has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It sets no cookies.
  • The only thing it stores in your browser is your light or dark theme choice, if you pick one.
  • What you send us through the contact form, by email or by phone is used to answer you and to do the work you ask for.
  • We don’t sell personal data.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time: email info@manpasand.com.

What this website collects

The website itself collects nothing about you. There are no analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising, no social media widgets and no cookies. Our fonts and styles are served from this site, so opening a page doesn’t contact Google Fonts or any other outside server.

Your theme choice

If you choose Light or Dark in the footer, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage, under the name “theme”. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. Choosing Auto removes it, and so does clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Hosting logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, whether or not you have a GitHub account. We don’t receive or look at those logs. GitHub’s handling of them is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

What you send us

The contact form asks for your name, email address, phone number and a description of what you need.

Right now, sending the form opens your own email app with a message addressed to info@manpasand.com. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app, and the message then travels like any other email, through your email provider and ours.

We plan to connect the form to Google Forms. When we do, your answers will be stored by Google in our Google account, under the Google Privacy Policy, and this page will be updated to say so.

If you email or call us instead, we receive what you send: your email address or phone number, your name and whatever you tell us about the job.

How we use it

We use what you send us to:

  • reply to you and set up a free consultation
  • write your scope of work and quote
  • do the work you ask for, and follow up on it, including the workmanship guarantee
  • keep ordinary business records of the work we do

We don’t use it for advertising, and we don’t add you to marketing lists.

Who else handles it

Only the services that carry it: GitHub, which hosts the website, and the email and phone providers that deliver your messages to us.

We don’t share your information with anyone else, unless you ask us to or the law requires it.

We don’t sell personal data

We don’t sell personal data to anyone, and that includes sensitive personal data as the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act defines it. We don’t share it for targeted advertising either.

Information we see while working for you

IT, network, database and web work can mean seeing your files, passwords, customer records or the inside of your home or office. We use that access only to do the work you asked for, and we keep it confidential.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and project records for as long as we need them to answer you, do the work, honor the guarantee and keep normal business records. When you ask us to delete your information, we do, except for anything the law requires us to keep, such as tax records.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email info@manpasand.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line, or call (715) 255-0420 (Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 5 PM Central).

We reply within one business day and tell you when it’s done. We may need to confirm it’s really you before we change or delete anything.

Children

This website is for adults arranging services. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13 through it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top of it.

Contact

Manpasand Technical Services, based in Rowlett, TX 75088. Email info@manpasand.com or call (715) 255-0420. Our terms of service cover the rest of how we work.
