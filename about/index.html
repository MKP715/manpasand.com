§ 04  What we don’t do

Where our work stops.

Knowing this up front saves you a call. If your project needs one of these, we’ll say so at the consultation.

Security cameras and alarms

We don’t install security cameras or alarm systems. Texas requires a DPS Private Security license for that work, and we don’t hold one. Hire a licensed company — you can check any installer at tops.portal.texas.gov.

Photos from the ground only

Every photo we take, inside and out, is taken from the ground: from the street, the driveway, the yard and the back patio. That’s where buyers stand at a showing, too.

Smart devices: the network only

Our smart-home work stays at the network level. We put TVs, speakers, thermostats and printers on their own network, walled off from your computers. See home network & Wi‑Fi security.
