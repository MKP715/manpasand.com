§ 08  Questions

Plain answers to common questions.

Something not covered here? Call or email us.

All FAQs
Q.01Is the first consultation free, and how do quotes work?

Yes, the first consultation is free. Call, email or use the contact form to tell us what you’re working with.

We then send a written scope of work and one fixed price, with any Texas sales tax shown as its own line, before any work begins. No hidden fees.

Q.02When are you available, and how fast do you reply?

Our hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM – 5 PM Central Time. Every message gets a reply within one business day, and critical support requests are acknowledged within two hours during those hours. We agree a start date with you when we send the quote.

Q.03Do you come on-site, or work remotely?

Both. Office IT, networks, Wi‑Fi, conference rooms and photography are on-site across Dallas–Fort Worth, from our base in Rowlett, TX 75088. Database and web work can be done remotely, wherever you are. See our service areas.

Q.04Do you work with homeowners as well as businesses?

Yes. We work with small businesses and with households. For homes, we set up home Wi‑Fi and network security, photograph homes for listings, take family portraits, and give households a plain-English second opinion through tech consulting.

Q.05Is there anything you don’t do?

We don’t install security cameras or alarm systems. Texas requires a DPS Private Security license for that work, and we don’t hold one. Hire a licensed company — you can check any installer at tops.portal.texas.gov.

We also don’t do electrical work such as new power circuits; that needs an electrician. Our cabling is office network cabling for computers, phones and Wi‑Fi.
