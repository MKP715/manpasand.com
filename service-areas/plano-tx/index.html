Plano, TX

IT support and home Wi‑Fi help in Plano

We’re based in Rowlett, about 27 minutes from the middle of Plano without traffic. Our IT support in Plano is on-site for home Wi‑Fi, home offices and small conference rooms; database work happens remotely. Plano is the farthest of our six service-area cities, so we’re upfront about the drive.

One in five Plano workers works from home

Plano is a corporate town. When the Legacy West development opened in 2017, NBC 5 reported that its corporate tenants, including Toyota’s new North American headquarters, brought more than 20,000 jobs. Toyota Motor North America is still headquartered in Plano, on Headquarters Drive.

Plenty of Plano’s work happens in spare bedrooms, too. In the Census Bureau’s 2024 American Community Survey, about 21% of Plano workers said they usually worked from home, against 12.9% statewide. The survey asks how people usually got to work, so someone in the office three days a week counts as a commuter. The number of Plano homes with a work desk is likely higher.

That work laptop often shares one network with smart TVs, game consoles and the kids’ tablets. If your employer owns it, its VPN and security settings are their IT team’s call, and we leave them alone. Our home Wi‑Fi help in Plano covers everything around it:

  • A network cable or mesh access point at the desk, so video calls don’t ride a weak signal through three walls
  • TVs, speakers, thermostats and printers on their own network, away from the work laptop
  • A guest network, so fewer people need your main Wi‑Fi password
  • The FTC’s router basics: WPA3 or WPA2, a new admin username and password, current router software, and remote management, WPS and UPnP turned off

Small firms, meeting rooms and the data behind them

Most Plano businesses are small, though. Census ZIP Code Business Patterns for 2023 count about 10,900 business locations with paid employees in six Plano ZIP codes, and 83% of them have fewer than 20 employees. About 2,100 are professional, scientific and technical firms: law, accounting, engineering, design, computer and consulting offices. ZIP 75024, around the corporate campuses on Legacy Drive, has the most locations of the six.

If your clients are large companies, your meeting room has two jobs: hosting calls on your own platform and joining clients’ calls on whatever they use. A USB camera bar and speaker that plug into a laptop work with any app the laptop runs; the catch is that someone has to bring the laptop and plug it in. A dedicated room system starts a meeting with one tap, but it’s set up around one main platform and joins the others as a guest. Our conference room setup page compares the two.

Then there’s the data: a database somebody set up years ago, or a spreadsheet that quietly turned into one. Database work on SQL Server, MySQL or PostgreSQL doesn’t need us in your building, so for a Plano firm there’s no drive at all. Planning an office move? Tech consulting helps most before the lease is signed, while you can still check what internet the suite can get.

On-site IT support in Plano, and what the drive means

Plano is the farthest of our service-area cities from Rowlett: about 27 minutes and 18 miles to the middle of the city without traffic, mostly on the President George Bush Turnpike, a toll road. Plano is wide. East Plano (75074) is about 21 minutes away; the Legacy area (75024) is about 32. Rush hour adds to all of those.

So here’s the honest fit. We come on-site in Plano Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 5 PM, and planned work suits the distance: a home-office setup, a Wi‑Fi rebuild, a meeting room, an office move. We acknowledge critical support requests within two hours during business hours, but that’s a reply, not a technician at your door. If you need someone on-site within the hour, a provider based in Plano may serve you better. Either way, the first consultation is free, and you get a detailed scope and a fixed-price quote before any work starts.

Two local things to know. Report a power outage to the company that owns the poles and wires, not the one that sends your bill. In Plano that’s usually Oncor: call 1-888-313-4747 any time, or text OUT to 66267 once you’ve signed up for MyOncor Alerts. Storm outages here can be large and slow to clear. At 3 p.m. on May 29, 2024, the day after a severe storm, Oncor still counted 10,025 Plano households without power, and a March 2025 windstorm cut power to more than 20,000 Plano residents, by Oncor’s count. A battery backup keeps the modem and router up through short flickers; our power outage guide goes further. And before paying for a faster plan, check your address on the FCC National Broadband Map to see which providers report service there.

All FAQs
Q.01Can someone set up my home Wi‑Fi in Plano?

Yes. We come to Plano homes Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 5 PM, to fix weak spots, add a cable or mesh access point where the desk is, put smart TVs and speakers on their own network and lock down the router. We’re based in Rowlett, so we book a time rather than drop in. The first consultation is free.

Q.02Do you make on-site visits in Plano?

Yes, Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 5 PM. We don’t have an office in Plano; without traffic it’s about 21 minutes from Rowlett to east Plano and about 32 minutes to the Legacy area. For planned work that’s no problem. If you need someone on-site within the hour, a provider closer to you may be the better call.

Q.03How do I secure a home office network?

Start with the router. The FTC recommends WPA3 or WPA2 encryption, a new admin username and password, up-to-date router software, and turning off remote management, WPS and UPnP. Then separate things: guests on a guest network, and smart TVs, speakers and thermostats on their own network so they can’t reach the work computer. If the laptop belongs to your employer, leave its VPN and security settings to their IT team.

Q.04Do you offer remote IT help?

Yes, for some jobs. For Plano clients, a locked-out account, a mailbox that won’t sync or a software problem can often be fixed remotely, in a session you start and can end at any time (small business IT support). Database and web projects can be handled remotely from anywhere. Wi‑Fi, cabling, PC hardware and meeting rooms are hands-on, so for those we come to you in Plano. We respond within one business day and acknowledge critical support requests within two hours during business hours.
