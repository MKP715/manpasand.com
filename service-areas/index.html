§ 02  On-site vs remote

What needs a visit, and what doesn’t

Networks, rooms and photo shoots happen where you are. Database and web work can be done from anywhere.

On-siteDallas–Fort Worth

We come to your home or office anywhere in the metroplex for the work that depends on the building: network cabling, Wi‑Fi coverage, meeting rooms, printers and photo shoots.

  • Small business ITOn-demand IT for small offices and shops: networks, POS systems, computers, printers and day-to-day tech support.
  • Network & cybersecurityFirewalls, segmented Wi‑Fi, guest networks and a walled-off network for smart devices, with every change explained.
  • Conference room AVCameras, microphones, displays and tidy cabling for small meeting rooms, set up for Teams, Zoom or Meet.
  • Home network & Wi‑Fi securityWhole-home Wi‑Fi, a locked-down router and a separate network for smart TVs, speakers, thermostats and printers.
  • PhotographyReal estate listing photos, headshots, corporate events, family portraits and product photos across DFW.
  • Tech consultingA plain-English review of your tech, vendors and spending, then a written plan you can act on.

RemoteAnywhere

Database and web work doesn’t depend on the building, so we can do it remotely for clients anywhere. If you’re in DFW, we can still meet on-site.

  • SQL database & ETLSQL Server, PostgreSQL and MySQL design, tuning and ETL, on-site in Dallas–Fort Worth or remotely anywhere.
  • Web developmentFast, mobile-friendly websites, WordPress, online stores and web apps for small businesses.
